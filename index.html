How to play

  1. Pinch to grabTouch your thumb and index finger together over a shape.
  2. Open to dropSpread your fingers to let go.
  3. Swing to throwLet go while your hand is moving and the shape flies.
  4. No camera?Click and drag with a mouse, or drag with a finger on a phone.
  • Face the camera with your palm toward it, about an arm's length away.
  • Good lighting helps a lot. Avoid a bright window behind you.
  • The blue ring shrinks as you pinch. When it turns solid, you're holding.
  • Keys: R restart, Esc levels, M sound, H help, L leaderboards
