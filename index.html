How to play

🤏Pinch to grabTouch thumb and index finger over a shape.
🖐️Open to dropSeparate your fingers to let go.
💨Swing to throwLet go while moving and it flies.
🖱️Mouse works tooClick-drag, or drag with a finger on phones.
  • Face the camera with your palm toward it, about an arm's length away.
  • Good lighting helps a lot. Avoid a bright window behind you.
  • The blue ring shrinks as you pinch. When it turns solid, you're holding.
  • Keys: R restart · Esc levels · M sound · H help · L leaderboards
